Three ways to see the proposed tunnel and the trains that would run through it. All three are renderings of a proposal: the tunnel and its trains don't exist yet.

Virtual ride

Ride in the cab of a CrossTowner X1 from the Metra Electric tracks near 18th Street, down into the proposed Clinton–Roosevelt Connector tunnel, out at Chicago Avenue and onto the Union Pacific North tracks to Clybourn. On Steven Can Plan.

Cab ride, Hyde Park to Rogers Park

Ride the whole X1 with a map that follows the train, jumping to any stop or place along the way. Over Chicago/Halsted it stops for a 360-degree view from above the station and from its platform: the buses, Bally's Chicago, Halsted Pointe, and jobs at Prairie Material.

3D flyover map

Fly along the proposed CrossTowner tunnel, the Red Line Extension, and new transit lines in Austin, Bogotá, Toulouse and Hamburg over real 3D buildings.
