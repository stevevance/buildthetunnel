What is CrossTowner?

Regional rail is the “missing middle” between CTA's ‘L’ service and Metra's commuter rail operations. Star:Line Chicago's proposal would connect all of Chicagoland with it, largely on existing infrastructure. The new piece is a tunnel, the Clinton–Roosevelt Connector Line, a proposed subway in Chicago. Six CrossTowner routes, X1 through X6, would run on the combined network.

Read the full proposal at buildthetunnelchicago.org.

What the tunnel changes for your trip

The CrossTowner Trip Planner compares two networks side by side. One is today's CTA, Metra, and Pace service. The other adds the CrossTowner routes and the CTA Red Line Extension, which is under construction. For each trip it shows the travel time, the route, and the walk to and from stations. It also reaches O'Hare's terminals and South Shore Line stations in Indiana.

Every trip is computed ahead of time with Conveyal R5, an open-source routing engine used by transportation planners. The methodology page lists the data sources, assumptions, and limitations.

A bigger labor market for O'Hare

O'Hare is one of the largest job sites in Illinois, and most of its workers live outside the city. CrossTowner would put about 48,500 more workers within a 60-minute transit commute of the airport.
