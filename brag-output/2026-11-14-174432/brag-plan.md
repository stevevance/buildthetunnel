# Brag plan: Build the Tunnel — Clinton–Roosevelt Connector flythrough

## What is this app?
A MapLibre 3D viewer that flies a pitched camera along Chicago's proposed Clinton–Roosevelt Connector subway, over real extruded buildings, stopping at each station to show the residents and jobs around it.

## The angle
The product is already a film: a camera that frames the whole line, dives to street level and rides stop to stop. The video is that flight, compressed from about a minute to 24 seconds, with the site's own station callouts (jobs, residents, Census tract) landing as the camera arrives. Nothing is redrawn — the map is the real viewer, baked frame by frame.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
The whole line seen from above — dashed purple through the Loop's street grid, red station dots, existing Metra lines in grey — with the title already on screen: "Clinton–Roosevelt Connector · Chicago's proposed subway". Then the camera tips over and dives.

## Key moments
- The dive from the map view down to the Metra Electric connection (teal note chip, verbatim from the site).
- Four station arrivals, each with the site's purple callout: Roosevelt (11,533 jobs), Taylor/Clinton (24,894), Union Station (West) (37,015), Randolph/Clinton (29,493). Jobs count up as the camera settles.
- The corner sweeps: east–west under Roosevelt, then the turn north under Clinton between towers.

## Outro
Camera pulls back up to the whole line. "Build the Tunnel" — 3.2 miles, 4 stations, 102,935 jobs in the station Census tracts — and the URL.

## User flow worth showing
Open the viewer → it frames the line and dives → it rides stop to stop, dwelling at each station with its callout. That is the whole video.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: civic infrastructure film; a drone flight over a subway that does not exist yet
- Interpretation: one continuous shot, no cuts; type is calm and lands still; the camera supplies the energy.

## Formats: landscape 1920x1080 and vertical 1080x1920 (the map is baked separately for each; the vertical is recomposed, not cropped)
## Duration: 24.5 s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: the OpenFreeMap "liberty" basemap the viewer uses
- Accent: #8d5a99 (line and callouts) · note colour #0e7c7b · station dots #d62728
- Text: white on accent; #2a1830 on the white panels (the site's panels are white at 90%)
- Display and body font: Inter (the site uses system-ui)
- Strongest visual element: the dashed purple line threading the extruded Loop

## Share copy (draft)
Chicago's proposed Clinton–Roosevelt Connector subway, flown in 3D: 3.2 miles, four stations, and 102,935 jobs in the Census tracts around them.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady, clean), from 0 s at 0.32, 0.4 s fade-in, 1.6 s fade-out
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (110 BPM). Camera arrivals sit on beats 6.56, 9.83, 13.11 (strong cue, Union Station — the largest number), 16.38; the dive starts on 2.19; end-card stats on 21.28 / 21.84 / 22.37; the jobs total lands on 22.93 (strongest cue).
- Audio-reactive treatment: none — the picture is a baked camera move and the type is meant to sit still; pulsing would fight both.
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft drop per station card, a soft impact as the dive begins, one bell on the final total.
- Restraint rule: no sound without a cause on screen.

## Storyboard (one continuous shot)

### 1 — The line — 0–2.2 s
Top-down view of the whole line. Title panel on screen from frame 0; kicker and sub-line settle in.
Transition: the camera itself → 2

### 2 — The dive — 2.2–4.4 s
Title leaves; camera tilts to 62° and glides down to the south end. Teal chip: "Connection to Metra Electric District".
Audio: soft impact at 2.19.

### 3 — Stop to stop — 4.4–19.5 s
Four legs, four holds. A station card arrives just ahead of each stop and leaves just after departure (about 2.4 s each): "Stop n of 4", name, jobs (count-up), residents, Census tract.
Sequential: four cards, one per stop, each with a drop sound, beat-gridded as above.

### 4 — Pull back and totals — 19.5–24.5 s
Teal chip "Low Line connection" at the north end; camera rises back to the map view. End panel: "Build the Tunnel", URL, then 3.2 miles · 4 stations · 102,935 jobs (count-up, bell at 22.93). Holds 1.6 s.

**Music mood:** steady, optimistic. **Audio summary:** a clean bed under one long move, a tick at each stop, one bell on the total.
