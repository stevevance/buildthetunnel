Metra's lines end at four downtown stations that don't connect, so changing trains means crossing downtown on foot. A 3-mile tunnel under Clinton and Roosevelt would join them, so trains run through to the other side of the region.

Northwestern to UChicago today: 69 minutes and 2 transfers, with 21 of those minutes spent getting between trains. With the tunnel: 44 minutes on one train.
