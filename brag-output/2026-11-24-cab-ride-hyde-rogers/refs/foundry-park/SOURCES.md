# Foundry Park: reference images and sources

`assets/foundry-park.js` draws JDL Development and Kayne Anderson's Foundry Park on the old A. Finkl & Sons steel works at Cortland St, built out as the plan has it: Phase 1 (Subarea D: the hotel, two brick apartment buildings and the 39-story tower round Central Square) and the later Subareas A, B, C, E and F. Everything comes from the City's 68-slide deck for the January 15, 2026 Plan Commission hearing (the amendment of Planned Development 1439). Every building's footprint, its tower and podium parts and its stories were traced from the enlarged master plans (slides 15-18, 0.46 m a pixel); Phase 1's footprints were traced from its plans (slides 38-44) as Chicago YIMBY republished them at twice the deck's resolution (0.23 m a pixel). Heights come from the deck's building data, massing views, elevations and sections; the streets from the infrastructure plan; the design from the renderings, facade axonometrics and materials board. The plan was placed on OpenStreetMap's street centerlines, river banks and Finkl lots (Phase 1 by six street intersections, within 3 m of the Finkl lot once drawn 3% shorter north-south), with the corrections listed in the module's header where the deck's drawing and OSM disagree. No openly licensed photographs exist of the unbuilt plan, and no analysis plot made without the deck's drawings survives, so every file is in `private/`.

Files in this folder are committed to the public repository: openly licensed images (Creative Commons, public domain, US government) and our own analysis images. Files in `private/` are kept on Steven's machine only (`private/` is gitignored) because they are news photos, developer or architect drawings and renderings, Plan Commission slides, or anything else whose license is unclear.

## Private (not committed)

The City's deck and its slides, Chicago YIMBY's copies of the developer's sheets and renderings, and our tracing overlays on the deck's drawings. The slides are rendered from the deck (11, 15-18 and 38-46 at 4000 px, the rest at 1467 px). Working crops cut from them for tracing, contact sheets of the deck, sRGB copies of YIMBY's images and the traced coordinates (JSON) are not kept.
