# Prairie Materials Yard 32: reference images and sources

`assets/prairie-material.js` draws Prairie Materials' Yard 32, the ready-mix concrete plant at 835 N. Peoria St., between the Union Pacific spur, the North Branch and the Halsted St viaduct. Every footprint and position comes from OpenStreetMap (November 2026): the site, the silo row, the batch plant, the shed, the transfer and head houses, the conveyors, the crane runway, the quay, the basins and the block walls. Every height and shape comes from Cook County's 2022 LiDAR (the surface model less the bare-earth model, on 2 m, 1 m, 0.5 m and 0.25 m grids and in swaths along the conveyors), with the yard's median grade, 180.0 m NAVD88, as y = 0: the 11 silos were fitted as circles (9.45 m across, 24.0 m roofs), and the batch tower's 42.5 m roof, the conveyors' climbs, the steel silos, the billboard's two faces and the barges were all read from the returns. The look comes from AlphaBeta135's September 2022 Commons photograph from the Halsted St bridge, Jack B. Siegel's 2018 Open House photographs (viewed for reference only), the 2019 NAIP aerial and Prairie's own logo file; the company facts (two central-mix plants, 4,000 cubic yards a day, sand and gravel by barge from Romeoville) come from prairie.com and the press. Colors are approximate, since the color photos are at sunset.

Files in this folder are committed to the public repository: openly licensed images (Creative Commons, public domain, US government) and our own analysis images. Files in `private/` are kept on Steven's machine only (`private/` is gitignored) because they are news photos, developer or architect drawings and renderings, Plan Commission slides, or anything else whose license is unclear.
