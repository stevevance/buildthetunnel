# Willis Tower: reference images and sources

`assets/willis-tower.js` draws Willis Tower (the Sears Tower) as SOM's bundle of nine 75 ft (22.86 m) square tubes, 69.80 m square at the outside faces and turned 1.0 degree counterclockwise from true north, each tube stopping at its real setback (the 50th, 66th, 90th and 108th floors). The footprint and grid come from the published dimensions (SkyscraperPage, SOM, Princeton's Fazlur Khan pages) placed on OpenStreetMap's tube parts. Every roof height, the penthouse, the antennas' collar and the antennas' and masts' positions were measured in Cook County's 2022 LiDAR (the ISGS surface model less the bare-earth model) above Franklin St grade (180.85 m NAVD88), the datum the published heights fit, and the floor levels were fitted to those setbacks and CTBUH's 108 floors. The curtain wall, the black louver bands, the crown, the antennas' 2026 shape, the Skydeck Ledge and the 2022 Catalog podium were drawn from openly licensed photographs on Wikimedia Commons, chiefly Julian Lupyan's August 2026 view from due south (a camera fitted to the roof edges in it gave the antenna tips and mast tops), AmyLExtraordinaire's view from Halsted St and Grand Ave (almost the Chicago/Halsted platform's bearing), Jaysin Trevino's 2013 antenna close-up and Warren LeMay's 2024 photograph of the west face. Colors were read off the same photographs. The module's header marks everything that was assumed rather than measured.

Everything here is openly licensed (Creative Commons, public domain) or our own analysis, so all of it is committed to the public repository; this model has no `private/` files.
