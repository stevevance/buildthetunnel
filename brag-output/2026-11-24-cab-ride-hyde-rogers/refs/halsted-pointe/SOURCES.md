# Halsted Pointe: reference images and sources

`assets/halsted-pointe.js` draws Onni Group's Halsted Pointe (Planned Development 1522) at the southern tip of Goose Island as the City's deck for the November 19, 2026 Plan Commission hearing presents it: five residential towers and a hotel on raised podiums round landscaped courts, with a riverwalk, a lawn and a beach. The towers' stories, heights and homes come from the deck's building data; the heights above Chicago City Datum were turned into heights above the raised terrace (+26 ft CCD) with the building sections, which also give the floor-to-floor heights. The level 3-7, level 8 and typical floor plans were traced and registered to OpenStreetMap's outline of the site (way 1187740901) by a least-squares fit to five corners (0.119 m a pixel at 300 dpi from the deck's own scale bar, turned 1.8 degrees, 1.2 m rms); the towers were set on their elevator cores and come within 3% of the deck's typical floor areas. The elevations give the crowns' steps and B2's and C2's roofs, the facade material sheet the materials, and the site and landscape plans the pools, lawns, beach, courts and drive. Phase 1 (Subarea A, Hartshorne Plunkard Architecture, under construction) comes from HPA's April 2023 design-revision sheets as Urbanize Chicago published them, checked against CTBUH.

Files in this folder are committed to the public repository: openly licensed images (Creative Commons, public domain, US government) and our own analysis images. Files in `private/` are kept on Steven's machine only (`private/` is gitignored) because they are news photos, developer or architect drawings and renderings, Plan Commission slides, or anything else whose license is unclear.
