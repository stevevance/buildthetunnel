Subject: See the kind of train that could run through the tunnel – free, Oct. 5–7 at McCormick Place

Hi,

You're getting this because you signed up for updates when you used the CrossTowner trip planner at Build the Tunnel.

Next week, Metra and Stadler are showing off the first battery-electric train built for the U.S. – the same model Metra will run on the Rock Island's Beverly Branch starting in 2028. As Star:Line pointed out on Bluesky, if you want to see the kind of train that could run through the tunnel, this is your chance.

WHERE: APTA EXPO, McCormick Place South Building, 2301 S. Martin Luther King Dr. Enter at the Level 1 Main Lobby, Gate 4.
Stadler is listed at booths 1013 and 1317.

WHEN: The expo floor is open Monday, Oct. 5, 10 a.m.–5 p.m.; Tuesday, Oct. 6, 9 a.m.–5 p.m.; and Wednesday, Oct. 7, 9 a.m.–3 p.m. Stadler and Metra will officially unveil the train Monday at 1:30 p.m. (Stadler's announcement: https://www.stadlerrail.com/en/media/media-releases/apta-expo-metra-flirt-battery)

COST: Free. Expo-only registration is free to the public.

HOW TO GET IN:
1. Register online first: https://www.aptaexpo.com/exporeg.cfm (click Start). The form is built for the transit industry, so here's how I filled it out:
   - Primary business: choose "Other" and type "Transit advocate"
   - Primary job function: choose "Other" and type "Transit advocate"
   - Primary business objective: "To network with industry peers"
   - Role in purchasing: "No Role"
2. Pick up your badge at the exhibit hall entrance on Level 3.
3. Bring a photo ID, just in case.

CAN'T MAKE IT? The Daily Herald reports that after the expo, Metra will display the train at one of its facilities, most likely LaSalle Street Station. No date has been announced yet: https://www.dailyherald.com/20260928/transportation/first-in-the-country-metra-to-showcase-game-changing-battery-operated-passenger-train/

ABOUT THE TRAIN: It's a Stadler FLIRT with two passenger cars and a battery "PowerPack." It runs 45 to 65 miles on a charge and recharges from 20% to 80% in about 20 to 30 minutes. Metra has ordered eight of them.

If you go, take photos – I'd love to see them.
