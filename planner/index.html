      Privacy

      Your trips are computed in your browser from precomputed data. To understand usage, we log each trip's stations and timing with an anonymous ID, plus how you arrived at this website and your device type. When a search finds no rail trip, we also keep the places you entered and their coordinates, to map where service is missing. We also count aggregate visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. Your email is not associated with your use of the planner, but if you provide feedback then your email is associated with the most recent planned trip.
